- Use original, project-owned text, layouts, colors and icons.
- Do not reproduce Rockstar, GTA VI, FiveM or Cfx.re logos as the project's mark.
- Do not use official promotional artwork, screenshots, textures, sounds, models or character images without a specific right to redistribute them.
