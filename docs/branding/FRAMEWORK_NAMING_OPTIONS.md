# Framework naming workshop — open decision

**Status:** shortlist only. **No name has been chosen or registered** and the repo/package names must not change until the maintainer chooses one.

## Criteria

- Independent of Rockstar, Grand Theft Auto, GTA VI, Vice City, Cfx/FiveM, ESX, QBCore and Qbox marks.
- Four to seven readable letters preferred; easy to pronounce in Spanish and English.
- Works with an unambiguous modifier such as `Core`, `RP` or `Framework`.
- Short import/module prefixes should be reserved **after** a name decision. The project needs an independent prefix, not `qb-`, `esx_` or `gta6` natives.
- No implication that the product is an authorized GTA VI runtime.
- Final choice requires EUIPO/Spanish OEPM trademark review in relevant software classes, plus GitHub organizations, npm namespaces, domain and social handles; a quick web search is **not** legal clearance.

**Working recommendation:** Trama Core or Senda RP are memorable and describe the project without relying on any publisher property. These are common dictionary words, not exclusive marks; availability and distinctiveness must still be checked.

## Screened ideas to avoid or investigate thoroughly

Quick public search on 24 Nov 2026 found existing projects/platforms or roleplay communities using Veyra, Velora, Rivora, Nexora, Civra, Vorla, Arqen, Kovra, Narvex and others. Avoid adopting these as though unused. This is not a complete trademark or company search.

## Identity hierarchy (after name approval)

- **Framework:** selected independent name (brand owned by project).
- **Repository:** `gta6-rp-platform` can retain its searchable historical URL during transition, clearly described as a *pre-runtime research and roleplay foundation*.
- **Website:** lead with the chosen framework brand; mention GTA VI only as a non-affiliated long-term aspiration.
- **Packages:** use a verified available namespace, versioned contracts, and documented resource-prefix policy.

Do not use Rockstar visual identity, logos, official game artwork, fonts, key art or claims of partnership.
