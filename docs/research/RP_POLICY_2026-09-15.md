# Rockstar Creator Platform / Mod Policy — September 2026

**State:** REVIEW REQUIRED

**Verified:** 2026-09-23

## Findings

**Classification:** `CONFIRMED` for Rockstar's current company-wide modding and Creator Platform policy. This is **not** a GTA VI community-runtime confirmation.

### Rockstar Mod Guidelines — published 2026-09-10

Rockstar Games published a first-party **Mod Guidelines** policy dated **September 10, 2026**. It materially clarifies the permitted boundary for Rockstar-game modifications and licensed multiplayer modding.

Exact claims supported by Rockstar's policy:

- multiplayer modding is limited to Rockstar Games' **official licensed Creator Platform**, unless otherwise licensed directly by Rockstar Games;
- modding outside permitted Creator Platform monetization must be **strictly non-commercial**;
- the Creator Platform is Rockstar's licensed channel for monetizing mods;
- real-money gambling, loot boxes/gacha, real-money virtual-currency sales, cryptocurrency/crypto assets, third-party advertising/sponsorship, and monetized access to Rockstar-created assets/missions/DLC are prohibited under the stated rules;
- mods must not expand or modify Rockstar's official storylines, plots, missions, characters or established continuity;
- mods must not combine Rockstar IP/assets across Rockstar titles;
- unofficial ports to hardware/platforms where Rockstar has not officially released the game are prohibited;
- cheat menus/software interfering with official multiplayer, online services or the licensed Creator Platform are prohibited;
- the guidelines are **not a license**, may be revised, and do not waive Rockstar/Take-Two enforcement rights.

Official source:

- Rockstar Games — _Mod Guidelines_, published 2026-09-10, verified 2026-09-23: https://www.rockstargames.com/community-resources/mod-guidelines

### RP-server support article — updated 2026-09-15

Rockstar Games Support updated **Policies and guidelines regarding Roleplay (RP) Servers** on **September 15, 2026**. It now explicitly states that Rockstar Games **allows RP Servers through its Creator Platform**, provided they comply with the linked policies and guidelines.

The article directly links:

- Rockstar Games Terms of Service;
- Rockstar Games Creator Platform License Agreement;
- Rockstar Games Mod Guidelines.

Official source:

- Rockstar Games Support — _Policies and guidelines regarding Roleplay (RP) Servers_, last updated 2026-09-15, verified 2026-09-23: https://support.rockstargames.com/articles/5I66kExWgligszgMCU3XC1/roleplay-rp-servers

## What this establishes

- Rockstar has an explicit current policy boundary for modded multiplayer: its licensed Creator Platform or a separate direct Rockstar license.
- Rockstar explicitly permits RP servers through that Creator Platform subject to its governing documents.
- Monetization, IP/content use, unsupported ports and interference with official multiplayer now have a consolidated first-party policy surface that the project must treat as a deployment/compliance dependency.

## What this does not establish

These policy documents do **not** confirm:

- that GTA VI is supported by the Creator Platform;
- a GTA VI Cfx.re/FiveM-equivalent client or runtime;
- GTA VI community-server binaries or hosting requirements;
- GTA VI APIs, natives, scripting languages, synchronization semantics, resource formats or authentication model;
- a GTA VI PC release;
- that current FiveM/RedM runtime behavior or licensing terms will be portable to GTA VI;
- official GTA VI mod support. The Mod Guidelines are company-wide policy evidence, not a GTA VI runtime/API announcement.
