# Rockstar Creator Platform RP Policy — 2026-09-15

**State:** REVIEW REQUIRED

**Verified:** 2026-09-23

## Finding

**Classification:** `CONFIRMED` for Rockstar's current Creator Platform policy. This is **not** a GTA VI community-runtime confirmation.

Rockstar Games Support updated its public **Policies and guidelines regarding Roleplay (RP) Servers** article on **September 15, 2026**. The article now explicitly states that Rockstar Games **allows RP Servers through its Creator Platform**, provided they comply with the linked policies and guidelines.

The first-party article directly links the governing surfaces:

- Rockstar Games Terms of Service;
- Rockstar Games Creator Platform License Agreement;
- Rockstar Games Mod Guidelines.

Official source:

- Rockstar Games Support — *Policies and guidelines regarding Roleplay (RP) Servers*, last updated 2026-09-15: https://support.rockstargames.com/articles/5I66kExWgligszgMCU3XC1/roleplay-rp-servers

## Exact claim supported

- Rockstar currently permits RP servers through the Rockstar Games Creator Platform subject to its policies and guidelines.
- The Creator Platform License Agreement is now directly identified by Rockstar Support as a governing document for permitted RP servers.
- The current RP-server policy surface is therefore more explicit than a generic statement that Rockstar merely recognizes third-party RP servers.

## What this does not establish

This policy update does **not** confirm:

- that GTA VI is supported by the Creator Platform;
- a GTA VI Cfx.re/FiveM-equivalent client or runtime;
- GTA VI community-server binaries or hosting requirements;
- GTA VI APIs, natives, scripting languages, synchronization semantics, resource formats or authentication model;
- a GTA VI PC release;
- portability of current FiveM/RedM behavior or licensing terms to a future GTA VI creator/runtime product.
