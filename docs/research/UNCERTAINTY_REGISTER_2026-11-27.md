# CALM product and runtime uncertainty register

**State:** ACTIVE · **Reviewed:** 2026-11-27 · **Owner:** CALM research (maintainer review required before changing a state)

This register covers questions that affect CALM's GTA VI roleplay requirements or a future authorized integration. It complements [`RUNTIME_REQUIREMENTS.md`](../server/RUNTIME_REQUIREMENTS.md), where each `RT-*` integration capability remains explicitly gated. It does not track PC compatibility projects, reverse-engineering progress, game-file formats or external-framework internals; that documentary research is maintained separately.

## Resolution classes

- `R-official` — requires a direct Rockstar, Take-Two or Cfx.re publication.
- `R-evidence` — may be resolved by attributable, repeatable observation of a released product, with version and conditions recorded.
- `R-time` — depends on a future product or public announcement; no implementation action can resolve it now.

## Standing rules

1. Change an entry only when its stated trigger is met; speculation and compatibility milestones do not close product questions.
2. When an official runtime fact changes, update [`PLATFORM_STATUS.md`](PLATFORM_STATUS.md) first, then affected `RT-*` requirements, this register and any necessary ADR.
3. Record observed gameplay with source, game version, conditions and limitations. Label campaign observations as such; do not project them onto a hypothetical online product.
4. Keep the framework useful while integration is unknown: develop and verify CALM-owned RP rules with synthetic data, and keep the game adapter fail-closed.
